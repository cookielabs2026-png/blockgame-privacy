Privacy Policy for Gridlox

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Gridlox ("the app", "we", "us") is a free puzzle game published by Cookie Labs. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress — scores, level, coins, power-ups, unlocked board skins and settings — is stored locally on your own device, and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Gridlox does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, or photos.

There is one exception to data staying on your device: the optional Weekly Cup leaderboard, described below. Everything else stays local.

Advertising

Gridlox is free and supported by ads. We use Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded advertisements. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain information, including a device advertising identifier, IP address, and app-usage/interaction data. This information is handled under Google's policies. You can learn more, and manage ad personalization, here: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Players who purchase the optional "Remove Ads" subscription will no longer be shown ads.

Analytics and crash reporting

To understand how the game is played and to find and fix bugs, Gridlox uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services collect app-usage events (for example, that a game was started or a level was reached), approximate location derived from IP address (country/city level), device and app identifiers such as the advertising ID and a per-install app-instance ID, and — if the app encounters an error — technical crash information such as the error message and device model. This data does not include your name or any content you enter, and we never sell it or share it with anyone other than Google, which processes it on our behalf under Google's privacy policy. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, analytics storage is kept OFF unless you accept the in-app consent message; declining it disables analytics for your device.

The Weekly Cup (optional leaderboard)

Gridlox includes an optional Weekly Cup: a leaderboard built on the Daily Puzzle. It is switched on by default, and you can turn it off at any time under Cup → Name & data. While it is off, nothing about your play leaves your device.

While it is on, finishing a Daily Puzzle sends that run to a server we operate using Google Firebase (Cloud Functions and Firestore, hosted in the United States). What is sent and stored is:

  • an anonymous player ID created by Firebase Anonymous Authentication. It is not linked to an email address, a Google account, or your real name;
  • an automatically generated cup name such as “Sunny Fox 42”, which you can reroll. You cannot type your own name, so you cannot accidentally publish your real one;
  • your score for that day, which days you played, and which pet you have equipped;
  • the list of moves you made in that run. Our server replays it to confirm the score is genuine, which is how the Cup stays fair without having to trust the app.

What other players can see. Your cup name, your equipped pet, your scores and how many days you played appear on the Cup standings inside the game. Nothing else about you is shown, and your anonymous player ID is never shown to anyone.

How long it is kept. Stored move logs are deleted automatically 30 days after the run. Your standings entries and cup name are kept while you take part.

Deleting it. Cup → Name & data → Delete my cup data removes your standings entries, scores, stored runs, rewards and cup name from our server, deletes the anonymous sign-in itself, and switches the Cup off. You can rejoin later; you will be given a new cup name and a new anonymous ID. Step-by-step instructions, and what is deleted or kept, are on our delete your data page.

To check that scores come from a genuine, unmodified copy of Gridlox installed from Google Play, the app uses Firebase App Check with the Google Play Integrity API. This tells us whether a request came from a legitimate install; it does not identify you.

In-app purchases

Gridlox offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs and an ad-removal subscription). Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing; we do not receive or store your payment details.

Children's privacy

Gridlox is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the ad services, please contact us and we will address it.

Data we collect directly

The only data Cookie Labs holds on a server of its own is what the optional Weekly Cup stores, listed in that section above: an anonymous player ID, an automatically generated cup name, Daily Puzzle scores and the move logs used to verify them. We never receive your name, email address or Google account, and nothing in the Cup is used for advertising.

The other processing described in this policy (ads, analytics, crash reporting and purchases) is performed by Google on our behalf, and we only ever see it as aggregated, non-identifying statistics. We do not sell data, and we do not share it with any party other than Google.

Changes to this policy

If the app or its data practices change, this policy will be updated and the new version posted at this same address before the change takes effect.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through the contact details listed on our Google Play Store listing for Gridlox.
