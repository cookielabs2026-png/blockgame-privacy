Privacy Policy for Sunny Side Café

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Sunny Side Café ("the app", "we", "us") is a free restaurant and cooking game published by Cookie Labs. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data we collect

None. Sunny Side Café does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking software of any kind. It does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress — stars and best scores for each day, coins, upgrades, sound settings and whether you have finished the tutorial — is stored locally on your own device in the app's private storage. This data stays on your device, is never uploaded to us, and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

Sunny Side Café contains no advertising. No advertising software is included in the app, and no advertising identifier is collected or used.

In-app purchases

Sunny Side Café contains no in-app purchases. No billing or payment software is included in the app, and we never receive or store payment details.

Network use and permissions

The game plays entirely offline, makes no network requests, and asks for no special permissions on your device.

Children's privacy

Sunny Side Café is intended for a general audience. Because the app collects no personal information from any user, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app or its data practices change, this policy will be updated and the new version posted at this same address before the change takes effect.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through the contact details listed on our Google Play Store listing for Sunny Side Café.
