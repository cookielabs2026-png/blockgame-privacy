Privacy Policy for Bug Blaster

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Bug Blaster ("the app", "we", "us") is a free merge-defense game published by Cookie Labs. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress — waves, turrets, coins, cores, upgrades, daily rewards and missions, purchases you have unlocked, and settings — is stored locally on your own device. This data stays on your device, is not uploaded to us, and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Bug Blaster does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, or photos.

Advertising

Bug Blaster is free and supported by optional ads. We use Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example, for double coins or a free turret); the game never shows ads you did not ask for. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain information, including a device advertising identifier, IP address, and app-usage/interaction data. This information is handled under Google's policies. You can learn more, and manage ad personalization, here: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Players who purchase the optional "Ad-Free Pass" receive ad rewards without watching videos.

Your consent choices

In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, Bug Blaster uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the game.

In-app purchases

Bug Blaster offers optional in-app purchases (core packs, a permanent coin bonus, and an Ad-Free Pass). Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing; we do not receive or store your payment details.

Analytics and crash reporting

To find and fix the things that go wrong, Bug Blaster uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. When the game crashes — or hits an error it recovers from — Crashlytics sends technical information about it: the error itself and where in the code it happened, a short trail of what the game was doing beforehand (for example “wave 12” or “relocate”), how far you had progressed, your device model, its Android version and the version of the game. Analytics records app-usage events such as the game being opened or a wave being reached, an approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level), and device and app identifiers such as the advertising ID and a per-install app-instance ID.

None of this includes your name or anything you type, and we never sell it or share it with anyone other than Google, which processes it on our behalf under Google's privacy policy. You can stop it entirely by uninstalling the game; resetting your advertising ID in Android settings breaks the link to the previous identifier.

Children's privacy

Bug Blaster is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the ad services, please contact us and we will address it.

Data we collect directly

Cookie Labs does not collect, store, or share any personal data on its own servers — we run none. The data processing described above (ads, purchases, analytics and crash reporting) is performed by Google. We do not sell data, and we do not share it with any party other than Google.

Changes to this policy

If the app or its data practices change, this policy will be updated and the new version posted at this same address before the change takes effect.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through the contact details listed on our Google Play Store listing for Bug Blaster.
