Delete your data — Block Puzzle Pets: GridLox

App: Block Puzzle Pets: GridLox  ·  Developer: Cookie Labs
Last updated: September 24, 2026

Gridlox has no accounts and no sign-up. The only data that ever leaves your device is what the optional Weekly Cup leaderboard sends, and you can delete all of it yourself, from inside the app, in a few seconds. No request, no waiting and no need to contact us.

How to delete it — in the app

  1. Open Gridlox.
  2. Tap Cup on the home screen (or Daily → Weekly Cup).
  3. Tap ⚙ Name & data at the bottom of the Cup screen.
  4. Tap DELETE next to “Delete my cup data”, then confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and also switches the Weekly Cup off. You can rejoin later; you would be given a new cup name and a new anonymous ID.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Anonymous player ID (Firebase Anonymous Authentication)Deleted — the sign-in itself is removed
Cup name (for example “Sunny Fox 42”)Deleted
Daily Puzzle scores and weekly standings entriesDeleted
Stored move logs from your runsDeleted — and in normal use these are removed automatically 30 days after each run
Cup rewards and claim recordsDeleted
Your game progress (score, level, coins, pets, board skins, settings)Kept on your device only — it is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Retention

Move logs are deleted automatically 30 days after the run they belong to, whether or not you ask for deletion. Everything else listed above is removed as soon as you use Delete my cup data. Nothing is kept after that, and we keep no backups of it.

If you cannot use the in-app button

If you have already uninstalled Gridlox or cannot reach the button, contact us using the developer contact details shown on the Google Play listing for Block Puzzle Pets: GridLox and we will delete your Weekly Cup data for you. Please tell us your cup name so we can find the right record — it is the only identifier we have, and without it we cannot tell your data from anyone else’s.
