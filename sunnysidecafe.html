Privacy Policy for Sunny Side Café

Last updated: September 21, 2026 (version 1.1)

Sunny Side Café ("the app", "we", "us") is a free restaurant and cooking game published by Cookie Labs. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress — stars and best scores for each day, coins, upgrades, purchases you have unlocked, Sunny's outfit, sound settings and whether you have finished the tutorial — is stored locally on your own device in the app's private storage. This data stays on your device, is never uploaded to us, and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Sunny Side Café does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.

Advertising

Sunny Side Café is free and supported by optional ads. We use Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one — to double a day's earnings, to borrow a helper for one shift, or for a round of free desserts. The game never shows ads you did not ask for. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain information, including a device advertising identifier, IP address, and app-usage/interaction data. This information is handled under Google's policies. You can learn more, and manage ad personalization, here: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Your consent choices

In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, Sunny Side Café uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the game's title screen.

In-app purchases

Sunny Side Café offers one optional in-app purchase, the Supporter Pack. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing; we do not receive or store your payment details.

Analytics

Sunny Side Café does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

Children's privacy

Sunny Side Café is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the ad services, please contact us and we will address it.

Data we collect directly

Cookie Labs does not collect, store, or share any personal data on its own servers — we run none. The data processing described above (ads and purchases) is performed by Google. We do not sell data, and we do not share it with any party other than Google.

Changes to this policy

If the app or its data practices change, this policy will be updated and the new version posted at this same address before the change takes effect. (Version 1.0 of the game had no ads or purchases and collected no data.)

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through the contact details listed on our Google Play Store listing for Sunny Side Café.
